# **Authenticated Partner (Special Access)**

There is no solace above or below. Only us. Small. Solitary. Striving. Battling one another. I pray to myself, for myself. Power is a lot like real estate. It's all about location, location, location. The closer you are to the source, the higher your property value. Competence is such an exotic bird in these woods that I appreciate it wherever I find it. For those of us climbing to the top of the food chain, there can be no mercy. There is but one rule: hunt or be hunted. Shake with your right hand, but hold a rock in your left. The road to power is paved with hypocrisy and casualties. Never forget that.

Money is the McMansion in Sarasota that starts falling apart after ten years. Power is the old stone building that stands for centuries. I cannot respect someone who allows himself to be conquered. Friends make the worst enemies. If you don't like how the table is set, turn over the table. Decisions based on emotion aren't decisions at all. They’re instincts, which can be bloody and dangerous. Proximity to power deludes some into thinking they wield it. But in the end, we are nothing more than what we choose to leave behind.

**(Beta Feature)** Generosity is its own form of power. After all, a gift given without expectation is a debt that must eventually be repaid. There are two kinds of pain. The sort of pain that makes you strong, or useless pain. The sort of pain that’s only suffering. I have no patience for useless things. Treading water is the same as drowning for people like us. We don't submit to terror. We make the terror. You can't purchase loyalty, but you can certainly lease it for a very long time. In the game of politics, you must learn to survive the fallout, rebuild from the rubble, and smile at the people who pushed you into the fire.
